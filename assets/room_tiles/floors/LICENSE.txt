Floor textures for per-room floors (ROOM_THEMES_PLAN.md D.5), from ambientCG
(https://ambientcg.com), licensed CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Downloaded 2026-11-09 as
the 1K-JPG sets; only the Color, NormalGL, Roughness, Metalness and
AmbientOcclusion maps are kept, with their original file names.

  Tiles107        https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tiles107        -> floor_medical.tres
  DiamondPlate003 https://ambientcg.com/view?id=DiamondPlate003 -> floor_engineering.tres
  Carpet012       https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Carpet012       -> floor_quarters.tres
  MetalPlates004  https://ambientcg.com/view?id=MetalPlates004  -> floor_command.tres

The .tres materials map one texture repeat to one 1.5 m tile with world-space
triplanar UVs, so they work on any floor mesh without its own UVs. Nothing uses
them yet: D.5 picks a floor per room type.
