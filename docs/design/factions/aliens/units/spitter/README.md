# Spitter

*Source: GDD Section 11.6.*

| Speed | Toughness | Grouping | Core Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slow-moderate | Fragile | Solo, or supports Fodder swarms | Ranged pressure |

## Role

Applies pressure from range rather than closing distance. Dies quickly if focused — the
counterplay is to prioritize it as a target the moment it's spotted, rather than let it sit back
and chip away at the squad indefinitely.

## Combat behavior

- **Attack type: hitscan** — instant, guaranteed to travel in a straight line if unobstructed.
  Not a projectile with travel time or arc, unlike a grenade throw.
- **Favors maximum distance/LOS opportunities.**
- **Can trail behind a Fodder swarm as a soft screen** — the swarm absorbs melee attention while
  the Spitter fires from behind it.
- **Combat bias:** maintain distance, retreat rather than engage at close range.

## Relationship to other systems

- 20% of [Nest](../../design-choices/spawn-nests.md) spawns.
- Pairs naturally with [Fodder](../fodder/) as the ranged component of a mixed group.
