# SMG

| Base Accuracy | Damage | Magazine Size |
|---|---|---|
| 35 | 8 | 12 |

## Role

**Highest base accuracy** in the roster, tied with the Battle Rifle, but the **lowest damage** —
the tradeoff is a large, 12-round magazine. It's built for cheap, reliable Shoot (1 AP, GDD Section
4.2) spam rather than a single big hit: it lands often, but a target walks away from any one shot
easily.

## Suggested class

[Support](../units/support/) — a Support soldier's turn is usually built around its abilities
(healing/buffs, Initiative manipulation) rather than maximizing weapon damage, so a weapon that
fires reliably and rarely forces a Reload mid-mission fits better than one that demands ammo
discipline.

## Relationship to other systems

- Highest base accuracy makes it the best weapon in the roster for Overwatch (GDD Section 4.2) —
  the flat −30% Overwatch penalty (Section 4.2's math) eats into accuracy proportionally less when
  the base number is already high.
- 12 rounds means Reload (1 AP) rarely competes with a Support soldier's other actions for
  activations within a single engagement, keeping its ability-focused kit uninterrupted by ammo
  management.
