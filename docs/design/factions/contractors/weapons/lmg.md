# LMG

| Base Accuracy | Damage | Magazine Size |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | 14 | 15 |

## Role

**Largest magazine in the roster (15)** and solid damage, offset by low base accuracy — second
only to the Shotgun's. It's a sustained-fire/attrition weapon: no single shot is especially likely
to land or to hit hard when it does, but it keeps firing longer than anything else before Reload
(GDD Section 4.3) becomes a factor.

## Suggested class

[Heavy Weapons](../units/heavy-weapons/) — the class's high Fitness/HP pool lets it stand in place
and feed a weapon built to keep firing rather than to land a precise opening shot, matching its
"high damage, slow, area-effect" GDD role.

## Relationship to other systems

- Low base accuracy pushes toward Aimed Shot (2 AP, Perception-only, GDD Section 4.6.2) over
  Shoot for the same reason the class doc already notes about Heavy Weapons: Reflexes only helps
  Shoot, and this weapon's low base number makes that gap sting more than it would on a
  higher-accuracy weapon.
- 15 rounds is generous enough that the magazine rarely runs dry mid-engagement, supporting the
  suppression/area-effect identity implied by the class's GDD role even though no explicit
  suppression mechanic is specified yet.
