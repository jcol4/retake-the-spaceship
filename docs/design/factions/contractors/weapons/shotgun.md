# Shotgun

| Base Accuracy | Damage | Magazine Size |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | 22 | 2 |

## Role

Highest damage per hit of any weapon in the roster, by a wide margin — offset by the **lowest
accuracy** and, by far, the **smallest magazine** (2 rounds). It's built around landing one
reliable, decisive trade rather than sustaining fire: two shots, then a mandatory Reload (GDD
Section 4.3), so every activation carrying it is either "close distance and fire" or "reload,"
with very little room for a third option before the ammo runs out.

## Suggested class

[Assault](../units/assault/) — the aggressive alternative to the class's baseline Assault Rifle.
The class's high Fitness (largest movement range, Section 4.6.3) is what makes the Shotgun's low
base accuracy tenable: it exists to get a soldier who can actually reach effective range into a
fight fast, not to be fired from a held position.

## Relationship to other systems

- Low base accuracy compounds badly with the Distance Penalty curve (GDD Section 4.6.1) at range,
  since nothing about the weapon itself models a falloff distinct from the other four — closing
  distance is the only lever that makes this weapon's accuracy competitive, not a weapon-level
  range stat.
- The 2-round magazine makes Overwatch (GDD Section 4.2) a weak pairing — a missed reactive shot
  leaves the soldier one round from a Reload-only activation, worse than with any other weapon in
  the roster.
