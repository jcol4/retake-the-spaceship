# Battle Rifle

*Semi-automatic rifle.*

| Base Accuracy | Damage | Mag Size | Reserve | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 35 | 10 | 3 | 12 (4 mags) | 15 |

## Role

**Highest base accuracy** in the roster (tied with the SMG), the **highest damage of the long guns**,
and the **most penetration (80)**, which also makes it the best armor-piercer (it ignores 3 points
of plate). It pays with the **lowest total ammo in the roster (15)**, carried as 5 mags of 3, and
with being **clumsy up close**. The long-range precision and anti-armor pick: it hits hard and
lands often from distance, but a soldier carrying it has the least room in the roster to spend
shots carelessly, and is in trouble once something gets close.

## Close quarters

While a hostile the carrier can see is within 3 tiles
([close quarters](../../../systems/close-quarters/)), the Battle Rifle takes **−80 on Shoot and
Overwatch** (and the Suppress break shot) and **−20 on Aimed Shot**, before the 99% cap.

This exists because penetration and the 99% cap made it the best gun at *every* range. Up close
there was no distance penalty, a Sniper's raw Shoot sat around 167, and a round went through three
Fodder in a corridor. Now a Sniper's engaged Shoot is ~87% in the open and ~47% behind heavy cover.
An Assault carrying one drops to ~50% / ~10%. The smaller Aimed penalty keeps "slow down and aim"
as its close-range answer, at an Aimed Shot's AP price.

## Suggested class

[Sniper](../units/sniper/) — high Perception is the accuracy contributor to every ranged action
(GDD Section 4.6.2), so pairing it with the roster's highest-accuracy weapon compounds rather than
wasting either stat. The scarce total ammo also reinforces the class's existing
"positioning-dependent, not spray-and-pray" identity from its low Fitness/movement range — a Sniper
is already built around making each activation count rather than repositioning and firing freely.

## Relationship to other systems

- Rewards Aimed Shot (2 AP) specifically — high base accuracy means even the lowest-odds headshot
  zone stays viable, and with the roster's smallest total ammo pool, every shot (Shoot or Aimed
  Shot alike) is competing for a genuinely scarce resource, sharpening the
  Shoot-vs.-Aimed-Shot choice (GDD Section 4.2) that the game is already built around.
- Of the five weapons, this one makes ammo discipline matter most across a whole mission — 15 total
  shots doesn't survive much undisciplined firing, and unlike every other weapon there's no deep
  reserve to fall back on if a soldier burns through it early.
