# Battle Rifle

*Semi-automatic rifle.*

| Range table | Damage | Mag Size | Reserve | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −30 / −25 / −20 at 1 / 2 / 3 tiles, 0 at 6–8, +5 at 9–18, −3/tile after | 10 | 3 | 12 (4 mags) | 15 |

Range table: aim added at each distance on top of the soldier's Aim
([accuracy](../../../systems/accuracy/)). Aim 60 against an open target: 35% at 2 tiles, 60% at 6,
65% from 9 to 18.

## Role

The **best long-range table** in the roster (+5 from 9 to 18 tiles), the **highest damage of the long guns**,
and the **most penetration (80)**, which also makes it the best armor-piercer (it ignores 3 points
of plate). It pays with the **lowest total ammo in the roster (15)**, carried as 5 mags of 3, and
with being **clumsy up close**. The long-range precision and anti-armor pick: it hits hard and
lands often from distance, but a soldier carrying it has the least room in the roster to spend
shots carelessly, and is in trouble once something gets close.

## Up close

Its table is **−30 at 1 tile, −25 at 2, −20 at 3**: a long rifle aimed at someone in its face.
An Aim 60 soldier has a 35% Shoot at 2 tiles and 50% with an Aimed torso shot. Stepping back to 6
tiles makes it 60%.

This exists because penetration and the old 99% cap made it the best gun at *every* range: shots
started at 113–167 before any modifier, so up close nothing could lower them, and a round went
through three Fodder in a corridor. It briefly carried a −80 "engaged" penalty
([close quarters](../../../systems/close-quarters/)); the [accuracy rework](../../../systems/accuracy/)
replaced that with this table, which judges the distance to the *target*, not who is standing
near the shooter.

## Suggested class

[Sniper](../units/sniper/) — the class built to hang back, which is where this table pays (every
class now rolls the same 55–65 Aim, so the pairing is about position, not a better eye). The
scarce total ammo also reinforces the class's existing
"positioning-dependent, not spray-and-pray" identity from its low Fitness/movement range — a Sniper
is already built around making each activation count rather than repositioning and firing freely.

## Relationship to other systems

- Rewards Aimed Shot specifically — at its best range (+5) an Aim 60 head shot is 30%, a torso
  shot 80%, and with the roster's smallest total ammo pool, every shot (Shoot or Aimed
  Shot alike) is competing for a genuinely scarce resource, sharpening the
  Shoot-vs.-Aimed-Shot choice (GDD Section 4.2) that the game is already built around.
- Of the five weapons, this one makes ammo discipline matter most across a whole mission — 15 total
  shots doesn't survive much undisciplined firing, and unlike every other weapon there's no deep
  reserve to fall back on if a soldier burns through it early.
