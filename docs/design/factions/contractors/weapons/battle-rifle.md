# Battle Rifle

*Semi-automatic rifle.*

| Base Accuracy | Damage | Magazine Size |
|---|---|---|
| 35 | 16 | 5 |

## Role

**Highest base accuracy** in the roster (tied with the SMG) and **tied-highest damage** (with the
LMG), paid for with the **second-smallest magazine** (5, ahead of only the Shotgun's 2). The
precision pick: it hits hard and lands often, but a soldier carrying it burns through ammo fast
if they're not deliberate about when they fire.

## Suggested class

[Sniper](../units/sniper/) — high Perception is the accuracy contributor to every ranged action
(GDD Section 4.6.2), so pairing it with the roster's highest-accuracy weapon compounds rather than
wasting either stat. The small magazine also reinforces the class's existing "positioning-dependent,
not spray-and-pray" identity from its low Fitness/movement range.

## Relationship to other systems

- Rewards Aimed Shot (2 AP) specifically — high base accuracy means even the lowest-odds headshot
  zone stays viable, while the small magazine makes each Aimed Shot's extra ammo cost (relative to
  Shoot) a real decision rather than a rounding error, sharpening the Shoot-vs.-Aimed-Shot choice
  (GDD Section 4.2) that the game is already built around.
- Of the five weapons, this one makes Reload timing (Section 4.3) matter most after the Shotgun —
  5 rounds doesn't survive much Aimed Shot use without a deliberate Reload window.
